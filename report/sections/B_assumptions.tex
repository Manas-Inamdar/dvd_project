\section{Assumptions and open points}\label{app:assumptions}
The assignment leaves several points open. The following choices were made by the project team; \textbf{they are our assumptions and have not been confirmed by the course staff.}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Gate leakage definition (A1).} The leakage of a gate or circuit is the total static current delivered by all sources at $V_{DD}$ (supply rail plus inputs held high), which equals the
        current absorbed at $0\,$V; both are measured in SPICE and agree. The ML side sums the predicted transistor currents with the same definition. It is the physically complete static current of the cell.
  \item \textbf{Gate transistor widths (A2).} In units of $L_{\min}=22\,\mathrm{nm}$: INV $W_n=1$, $W_p=2$; NAND2 $W_n=2$, $W_p=2$; NOR2 $W_n=1$, $W_p=4$; AND2/OR2 use the NAND2/NOR2 sizes plus the INV.
        This is the usual convention (PMOS about twice the NMOS to balance the lower hole mobility, series stacks doubled) and lies inside the $1$--$8\times L_{\min}$ range of the training data.
  \item \textbf{Composition of the gate leakage (A3).} Kirchhoff-consistent composition: the channel current of each ON transistor is an unknown fixed by the current law at the internal nodes of the gate, gate and body currents come from the models, and the leakage is summed at the rails
        (Section~\ref{sec:gates_method}). The plain sum of the predicted terminal currents, which would be the naive reading of the assignment, is reported as a baseline and is unusable.
  \item \textbf{Dataset size (A4).} The delivered datasets have $10{,}000$ rows per case as specified. As the assignment allows the number of samples to be increased, $30{,}000$ additional training-only samples per case were drawn from the same distributions; validation and test sets are the original ones.
  \item \textbf{Parity checker (design choice).} Balanced tree of seven XOR2 cells, each built from four NAND2 gates (28 NAND2, 112 transistors). In the circuit-level ML prediction the SPICE-recorded node voltages of the isolated NAND2 of the same input combination and corner are used (the assignment's step 7).
  \item \textbf{Junction geometry.} $AD=AS=W\cdot L_{diff}$, $L_{diff}=44\,\mathrm{nm}$ (course-group clarification); PD/PS not set (adding PD$=$PS$=W+2L_{diff}$ changed only $I_b$, by about $10\%$ at the $10^{-12}$\,A level, at one test point).
  \item \textbf{Noise floor and error definition.} Currents below $10^{-15}$\,A are numerical noise of the reference simulator; relative errors $|I_{ML}-I_{SPICE}|/|I_{SPICE}|$ (median, 95th percentile, mean signed bias) are reported on currents above this floor.
  \item \textbf{SPICE reference validity.} Reference values are the mandated-option solution where it agrees with a tight-tolerance cross-check, otherwise the cross-check solution (Section~\ref{sec:gates_method}).
\end{enumerate}
